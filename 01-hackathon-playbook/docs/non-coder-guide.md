# 🧭 Non-Coder Guide: How to Carry a Hackathon Team Without Writing Code

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

> Judging criteria reward the problem, the user experience, the demo and the pitch as much as the code. A teammate who owns those is a full contributor. Pair this with the "Not a coder" learning path in the root README.

## Pick a role (or two)

| Role | You own | Deliverables | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Researcher** | The problem is real | 5-10 quick user conversations at the venue or online; a one-paragraph problem statement with a real quote | [`research.md`](../templates/research.md) |
| **Product lead** | Scope | The PRD, the must/nice/cut lists, saying "no" to new features after the freeze | [`prd.md`](../templates/prd.md) · [`problem-selection.md`](../templates/problem-selection.md) |
| **Designer** | How it looks and flows | Wireframes before coding starts, then 3-5 key screens (Figma free tier works) | [`02-frontend/`](../../02-frontend/README.md) |
| **Pitch lead** | The story | Slides built alongside development, the script, rehearsals, Q&A prep | [`pitch-and-demo.md`](pitch-and-demo.md) |
| **QA / tester** | It works on stage | Testing the demo path after every deploy, the bug list with priorities | [`demo-day-testing.md`](../../03-backend/docs/demo-day-testing.md) |
| **Submission owner** | Nothing is missed | Rules, README, video, form fields, repo access, sponsor requirements | [`mvp-and-demo-checklist.md`](../templates/mvp-and-demo-checklist.md) |

AI tools let non-coders prototype too (no-code builders, AI coding assistants), but disclose them as the rules require ([section 1](rules-hardware-a11y-remote.md#1-ai-use-originality-pre-existing-code-and-ip-allowed-but-undisclosed-use-is-what-gets-teams-disqualified)).

## Working with the developers

- Agree on the core flow in the first two hours and write it down; don't change it after.
- Hand over designs in small pieces as they're ready, not all at the end.
- Batch your questions; protect the builders' focus time.
- Test what they ship as soon as it's deployed and report bugs with steps to reproduce.

## Checklist

**Before:** research the theme; set up Figma and a slide template; read the rules.
**During:** validate the problem before building; wireframe before code; build slides as you go; test continuously; capture screenshots for the pitch and your portfolio.
**After:** write a short case study for your portfolio (problem, your role, what you did, result); update your CV ([`after-the-event.md`](after-the-event.md)); stay in touch with your team.
