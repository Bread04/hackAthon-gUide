# 🤝 Sponsor Tracks: Fit, Integration and Follow-up

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

> Sponsor prizes are often easier to win than the overall prize, because fewer teams use the sponsor's technology well. Use this when choosing an idea ([`problem-selection.md`](../templates/problem-selection.md)) and before the demo.

## Decode the brief

| Brief says | Usually means |
| --- | --- |
| "Leverage our AI / API" | Use our product for a real feature |
| "Showcase innovative use cases" | Something we can show in a blog post or to our VP, not a to-do app with our logo |
| "Enterprise-ready" | Something that solves a problem our customers have |
| "Seamless integration" | The integration should look deliberate, not bolted on |

What sponsors want in practice: **demoable in 30 seconds, on-brand for their industry, technically interesting, with a story they can retell.**

**Ask the sponsor (in person or on Discord) early:**
- "What would make a winning project stand out to your team?"
- "Are there use cases you hope to see, or ones you've seen too often?"
- "Will your engineers be available for questions during the event?"

Red flags: no stated prize, "build anything", or a brief that needs their free tier to do something it can't.

## The sponsor-fit check

Score your idea on two axes before committing:

| | Uses their tech deeply | Uses their tech lightly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Matches their business** | ✅ Go for the sponsor prize | ⚠️ They won't see enough of their product |
| **Doesn't match their business** | 🟡 Possible technical prize | ⛔ Skip this sponsor |

**Integration depth:** surface (logo, one call) → functional (one real feature) → **core (the project depends on it)** → showcase (a use they haven't seen). Aim for core: make their technology the hero of one feature, not one of ten.

## At the demo

- Say one sentence on why you chose their technology and what it enabled.
- Show the feature that uses it, live.
- Bring the sponsor's judges to the moment their product matters.

## Mistakes that annoy sponsors

- **Token integration:** an unrelated project with one API call to qualify.
- **Not reading the docs** before asking.
- **A broken demo** at their booth.
- **Disappearing:** asking for help, then never showing what it led to.
- **Generic follow-up:** "thanks, here's our project". Reference the specific help you received.

## After the event

Message the sponsor contact within a few days with your repo, demo video and one line on what their help enabled. This is how hackathon projects turn into internships, credits or partnerships. See [`after-the-event.md`](after-the-event.md).
