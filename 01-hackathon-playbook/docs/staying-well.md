# 🌙 Staying Well: Sleep, Energy and Knowing When to Stop

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

> A tired team makes worse decisions and more bugs. General wellbeing advice, **not medical advice**; follow the event's code of conduct and ask organisers for help if you or a teammate feel unwell.

## Sleep

- **Plan sleep into the schedule.** Going to bed at midnight and finishing at 6 a.m. beats coding through the night exhausted. Stagger sleep so someone is always awake if you must.
- **Short naps** (around 20 minutes, with an alarm) help alertness; avoid napping late in the evening if you want to sleep that night.
- **Caffeine:** stop by mid-afternoon if you intend to sleep; it will not replace sleep.
- Do the risky work (deploys, big merges) when people are rested, not at 4 a.m.

## Energy and body

- Drink water regularly; eat real meals, not only snacks and energy drinks.
- Stand up and walk every hour or so; rest your eyes from the screen (look at something far away for a few seconds).
- Use a proper chair and an external keyboard/mouse if you have them.

## Mind and team

- Say early if you're overwhelmed; it is a team problem, not a personal failure.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Organisers and the code of conduct are there for harassment, safety or health concerns. Know where to find them and the emergency contacts on day one.

## Pivot, shrink or stop?

Warning signs: stuck on the same core problem for many hours, a flaw in the main idea that you can't work around, nobody can explain the project any more, or the team has run out of energy.

1. **Shrink first:** cut to the smallest version that still shows the core idea. A simple working demo is still worth presenting.
2. **Pivot early** (in the first third of the event), not at the end.
3. **Stopping is allowed.** Tell your team directly; you can still attend judging and network.

## After the event

Sleep properly before driving or travelling, catch up on rest for a day or two, then do the retrospective ([`after-the-event.md`](after-the-event.md)).
