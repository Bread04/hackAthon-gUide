## Red flags (drop or shrink the idea if any is true)

- [ ] Needs data we cannot get before the event ends
- [ ] Needs more than one hard unsolved piece (new model **and** new hardware **and** new UI)
- [ ] The demo depends on a service with strict rate limits or sign-in flows
- [ ] We cannot say who uses it
- [ ] It is a thin wrapper around a single hosted AI tool (judges may call this a reskin)
